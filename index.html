What is this tool for?

Create strong, random passwords directly in your browser. No sign-up or installation is required. Everything happens on your device, so the generated password is never sent to any server or stored anywhere.

Check whether your password is truly secure. It is a simple way to spot weak, short, or predictable passwords before they become a risk.

Using long, random passwords is an effective way to protect your accounts. A 16-character password with uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols is recommended. Make your passwords unbreakable!
